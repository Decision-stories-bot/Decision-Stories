Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

This policy explains what data Decision Stories ("we", "us") uses, why we use it, and what choices you have. Decision Stories is a small creator project based in the Netherlands. We make interactive horror stories for Instagram, YouTube and TikTok (@decision.stories). This policy covers this website and our private posting tool, which appears on Google's sign-in screen as "Decision Stories".

1. The short version

  • This website collects nothing. It has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no forms and no scripts.
  • Our posting tool works only with our own accounts. It signs in to our own YouTube channel and nobody else's.
  • The only YouTube data it uses is from our own channel: our videos and the comments under them.
  • We never sell, rent or share data, and we never use it for advertising.

2. This website

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising tools, and does not run any scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs; we do not receive those logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. YouTube API Services

Our posting tool uses the YouTube API Services. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and the use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.

With the permission of our own YouTube channel, the tool:

  • uploads our own videos and sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails;
  • posts comments under our own videos (for example "Vote here") and replies to comments under our own videos;
  • reads the public comments under our own videos, to count the A/B votes for the next part and to answer them.

4. What data the tool uses

  • Access to our own channel. Google gives our tool an access token for our own YouTube channel. It is stored only on our own computer, inside our automation software, and is never shared or published.
  • Public comments under our own videos. The comment text, the comment ID, the commenter's public display name and channel ID, and the time it was posted. We use these only to count one vote per person for A or B, to reply to the comment (for example to confirm a vote or to say that a new part is out), and to make sure we never reply to the same comment twice.

The tool does not access any other channel's private data, and it does not access your email address, subscriptions, watch history or anything else outside our own channel.

5. How we process it

All processing happens on our own computer. To sort comments (a vote, a question, something else) and to suggest short replies, we use an AI model that runs on our own computer. Comment data is not sent to any third-party AI service, and we do not use YouTube data to train or improve AI or machine-learning models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Decision Stories' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In practice:

  • we use data from Google APIs only for the features described on this page;
  • we do not transfer it to anyone, except when the law requires it;
  • we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it;
  • only we, the owners of the channel, see it.

7. Instagram and TikTok

On Instagram, the tool uses Meta's Instagram API with our own professional account to publish our Reels and their covers, and to read and reply to comments under our own posts. It uses the same kind of data, for the same purposes and under the same rules as on YouTube. See the Instagram Privacy Policy. The tool does not connect to TikTok; we post there by hand.

8. How long we keep data

  • Vote totals (for example "A: 812, B: 640") and the IDs of comments we have answered: for as long as the story runs. They contain no comment text and no names.
  • Comment text and names in our working files: kept only as long as we need them to count votes and reply, and deleted on request (see below).
  • Our channel's access token: until we remove the tool's access.

9. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Vote totals may be shown in our videos (for example "Most of you voted A"). Our videos and comments are published through YouTube and Instagram, so those platforms receive what we post.

10. Security

Access tokens are kept only on our own computer, never on this website or in its code. Only we can use the tool.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your comment. You can delete your own comment on YouTube or Instagram at any time. A comment that is deleted before the vote closes is not counted.
  • Ask us to see or delete your data. Email decision.stories2001@gmail.com with your YouTube or Instagram name. We answer within 30 days and delete what we hold about you.
  • Revoke Google access. If you ever granted our tool access to a Google account, you can remove that access at any time on your Google Account's third-party access page (also reachable through Google security settings). Only our own account has granted it access.
  • Your rights in the EU. Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data, and you can object to how we use it. We process public comments based on our legitimate interest in running the vote and answering comments on our own videos. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

12. Children

Our stories are horror and are not made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.

14. Contact

Email decision.stories2001@gmail.com, or send a message to @decision.stories on Instagram.
