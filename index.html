How it works

  1. Watch a part. Every part ends on a choice between two options, A and B.
  2. Vote in the comments. Write A or B under the video.
  3. The most votes decide. The next part follows the option that won.

About

Decision Stories is a fan-made project: interactive horror stories with stick figures, made with our own tools. Each story comes out in short parts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, and the viewers decide where it goes.

Our posting tool

We use a small private tool to publish our videos. It works only with our own accounts. On our own YouTube channel it uploads our videos, and it reads and posts comments under our own videos to count the A/B votes and answer them. It is not offered to other people and it never signs in anyone else. Read the privacy policy for what data it uses and why.
